About Afkeenna

Waa Maxay?

What is it?

Afkeenna waa app loogu talagalay in la ilaaliyo afka iyo dhaqanka Soomaalida — gaar ahaan qurbo-joogta iyo dhalinyarada. Ma aha oo keliya qaamuus; waxay isku daraysaa casharo, ereyo cusub, iyo kayd dhaqameed oo ay bulshadu wax ku darto.

Afkeenna is an app dedicated to preserving the Somali language and culture — especially for the diaspora and younger generations. It's not just a dictionary; it combines lessons, new/dialect vocabulary, and a community-built heritage archive.

Sidee u Shaqaysaa?

How does it work?

Qaamuus — ereyo cusub iyo kuwo af-dhaqameed, aan ka iman qaamuuska rasmiga ah (fiiri qaybta Maktabadda).
Casharo — casharo fudud oo lagu barto alifbeetada, salaanta, iyo hadalka maalinlaha ah.
Kaydka Dhaqanka — maahmaahyo, sheekooyin, iyo caadooyin dhaqameed sida aroosyada iyo xeerarka, oo bulshadu ku darto.
Maktabadda — qaamuusyada rasmiga ah ee taariikhiga ah, oo la keydiyay si aanay u lumin.

Qaamuus — new and dialect words not found in the official dictionary (see Maktabadda for that).
Casharo — simple lessons covering the alphabet, greetings, and everyday speech.
Kaydka Dhaqanka — proverbs, stories, and traditions like weddings and customs, added by the community.
Maktabadda — the historic official dictionaries, mirrored here so they're never lost.

Sidee Wax Loogu Daraa?

How to contribute

Isticmaal badhanka "+" ee ku yaal Qaamuuska ama Kaydka Dhaqanka si aad u darto eray, maahmaah, sheeko, ama caado dhaqameed aad taqaan. Wixii aad ku darto waxay ka caawin doonaan qof kale inuu wax bartaa ama xasuusto.

Use the "+" button on the Qaamuus or Kaydka Dhaqanka tabs to add a word, proverb, story, or custom you know. What you add helps someone else learn or remember it.

Dictionary

Halkan waxaan ku ururinaynaa ereyo cusub, kuwo af-dhaqameed, iyo ereyo Soomaali ah oo laga yaabo in Carabi lagu bedelay — si aanay u lumin. For the official 2012 dictionary, see qaamuuska.org. This space is for new words, dialect terms, and original Somali words being displaced by Arabic borrowings — so they aren't lost.
